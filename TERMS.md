## 1. What the extension is for

Flow Recorder is a debugging and quality-assurance tool. It records network traffic, console errors, WebSocket and Server-Sent Event messages, page interactions, screenshots and screen video from browser tabs you choose, and stores the results on your device. It can export recordings as JSON, HAR, HTML reports and generated Playwright/Cypress test scripts.
